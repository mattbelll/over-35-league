Whether you're a returning skater or getting back into the game after a few years off, the Over 35 League at Carolina Ice Palace is always looking for players. Here's how to get involved.

## Who can play

The league is open to any hockey player **aged 35 and over**. Skill levels vary from players easing back into the game to seasoned skaters — the league keeps rosters balanced so games stay fun and competitive.

## Two ways to sign up

- **Full team** — already have a group? Register as a team and claim your spot in the league.
- **Free agent** — no team, no problem. Sign up on your own and the league office will place you with a team that needs skaters.

## What to expect

Every season includes a full slate of regular-season games across two sheets of ice — the Palace and Royal rinks — followed by a single-elimination playoff bracket seeded by the final standings. Goalies of any level are always welcome and often play at a discount.

Ready to get on the ice? Head to the [registration page](/registration) to grab your spot, or [reach out to the league office](/contact) with any questions.
