Walking into a hockey shop for the first time can feel overwhelming. Here's the honest, budget-friendly rundown of what you actually need for your first season in the Over 35 League.

## The essentials (non-negotiable)

1. **Helmet with cage or shield** — required for every player, no exceptions.
2. **Skates** — get them fitted at a shop and baked if possible. Comfort beats brand.
3. **Gloves, shoulder pads, elbow pads, shin guards, and hockey pants.**
4. **Mouthguard** — cheap insurance for your teeth.
5. **A stick** — ask a shop staffer about flex and height for your size.

## What you can rent or borrow at first

- Full equipment sets are available to rent at Carolina Ice Palace for your first few sessions.
- Many teammates keep spare gear — ask around your team before buying everything new.

## A few pro tips

- **Buy used for your first season.** Marketplace listings and shop clearance racks are full of lightly used gear.
- **Bag it right.** Air your equipment out after every skate — future you (and your car) will thank you.
- **Protect the important stuff.** A quality helmet and a good mouthguard are worth spending on.

You don't need top-of-the-line equipment to have a great first season. Get the safety basics right, show up, and the rest will follow.
