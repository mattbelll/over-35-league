Twelve games into the Summer 2026 season, the Over 35 League standings are about as tight as they get at the top — and there's still everything to play for heading into the final stretch.

## A logjam at the top

Three teams are tied on **16 points**: the **Dire Wolves**, **Worldvest** and **Holy City**. Goal differential currently separates them in that order, but any of the three could grab the top seed before the regular season ends.

- **Dire Wolves** — a league-best defensive record keeps them in first.
- **Worldvest** — the reigning Winter 2026 champions own the league's most explosive offense.
- **Holy City** — quietly efficient, right in the mix all season.

## The chase

**Shade-FOG** sit fourth and have shown they can hang with anyone, while **Kingspan** and **Burns Alley** are knotted together fighting for the middle seeds. **Revelry** and **BAIRD** round out the table and would love nothing more than to play spoiler down the stretch.

## What's next

The regular season wraps up over the coming weeks before the playoffs open on August 11 with a single-elimination bracket seeded by the final standings. Check the [schedule and standings](/schedule) for the latest results — and we'll see you at the rink.
